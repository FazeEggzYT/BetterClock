# Better Clock v1.4.5 - Text effects

## New
- **Outline** around the numbers (colour and thickness).
- **Gradient** colour on the numbers (end colour and direction).
- **Glow** on the numbers (colour, size, strength).
- **Shadow** settings: colour, blur, distance, direction, opacity.
- **Letter spacing** and **line height**.
- **UPPERCASE / lowercase** for the label, text above and below, date and extra lines.
- **Your own font file** (.ttf or .otf).
- **Separator** choice: colon, dot, dash, space or none.
- **Leading zero** on or off (05:00 or 5:00).

Outline, gradient, letter spacing and line height work with the Normal and Terminal number styles. Glow, separator and leading zero work with every style except the analog face.
