# Better Clock v1.4.3 - Settings polish

## New
- **Presets tab:** a theme gallery with 10 ready-made looks (Neon, Minimal, Glass, Retro green, Scoreboard, Gold, Ice, Sunset, High contrast, Default) and your own saved presets.
- **Tray menu:** Themes and My presets for quick switching.
- **Search box** on the Clock, Timer, Countdown and Settings tabs.
- **Reset this section** button at the bottom of every section.
- **Hover tips** on the settings.
- **Resizable settings window** that remembers its size.
