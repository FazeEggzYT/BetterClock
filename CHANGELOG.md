# Changelog

## v1.4.8
- New: share your look as a short code, or as a file you can import.
- New: Randomise button for a surprise look.
- New: 15 more themes (25 in total).
- New: Undo and Redo for your look (buttons or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
- New: "Same look on all three overlays" option.
- New: stack the parts side by side, and align them left, centre or right.
- New: fixed width, fixed height and a minimum width.
- New: snap to a grid and to the screen edges when you drag an overlay.
- New: compact settings that hide the help lines.

## v1.4.7
- New: the numbers can fade, slide, flip or pop when they change.
- New: idle breathing (the glow or the numbers slowly grow and shrink).
- New: countdown pulse speed and size.
- New: more appear animations: zoom, drop and bounce.
- New: choose how it disappears (fade, slide, shrink or none).
- New: animation speed and easing.
- New: shake or flash when a countdown is almost out.
- New: colours glide from one state to the next.
- New: animated (turning) gradient background.
- New: switch animations off for one overlay.
- New: every state (running, paused, low time, almost out, zero, overtime, target reached) can have its own background, border, glow and size.
- New: a night look that switches on by the clock.
- New: preview buttons for every state, plus copy and reset for each state.

## v1.4.6
- New: gradient with a third colour in the middle.
- New: round (radial) gradient background.
- New: a different roundness for each corner.
- New: shapes: circle, hexagon and slanted (plus the rounded box and pill).
- New: gradient border and border opacity.
- New: border lines: solid, dashed, dotted and double.
- New: inner glow along the inside edge.
- New: glass shine on the top half.
- New: texture over the background: noise, stripes, dots or a grid.
- New: colour and position for the shadow around the overlay.
- New: picture zoom, position and a colour tint.

## v1.4.5
- New: outline around the numbers (colour and thickness).
- New: gradient colour on the numbers.
- New: glow on the numbers (colour, size and strength).
- New: shadow settings (colour, blur, distance, direction and opacity).
- New: letter spacing and line height.
- New: UPPERCASE / lowercase for the label and the small texts.
- New: use your own font file (.ttf or .otf).
- New: choose the separator (colon, dot, dash, space or none).
- New: leading zero on or off (05:00 or 5:00).

## v1.4.4
- New: numbers, label, text above, text below, date, extra lines and icon each have their own font, size, colour, boldness, opacity and position.
- New: show or hide every part separately.
- New: choose the order of the parts and the space between them.
- New: colour and opacity for the progress ring or bar.
- New: icon colour and turn.
- New: "Copy this section to the other overlays" button.

## v1.4.3

- New: Themes and My presets in the tray menu.
- New: search box on the Clock, Timer, Countdown and Settings tabs.
- New: "Reset this section" buttons.
- New: hover tips on the settings.
- New: the settings window is resizable and remembers its size.

## v1.3.0
- Update from inside the app: download, install and restart by itself (tray menu, Settings > Updates, or automatically at start).

## v1.2.0
- Number styles: Normal, Flip-clock, LED seven-segment, Retro terminal, Analog clock face (clock only).
- Blinking colon option.
- Custom text above / below the numbers, optional day name and date lines.
- Small icon next to the numbers (built-in or your own picture).
- Picture / logo behind the numbers with opacity and fit.
- Drag the overlays with the mouse (Move mode, Ctrl+Alt+M or tray menu), even onto another monitor.
- Exact X / Y position boxes next to the 3x3 grid.
- "All monitors" option, and each overlay can use its own monitor.
- Games tab: per-game looks that switch on while the program is running.
- Update check: tells you when a newer GitHub release exists. Releases are built automatically by GitHub Actions.

## v1.1.0
- New **Changelog** tab (opens by itself the first time after an update).
- New **Settings** tab: dark / light / follow-Windows theme, accent colour, animations on/off + speed, UI size, startup options, export / import / reset.
- **Clock, Timer and Countdown each have their own tab** with their own look (font, colours, background, border, glow, size) AND their own position / monitor. All three can be on screen at once.
- Per-mode show animation (Fade, Slide, Pop, None).
- Animated UI: window open, tab changes, switches, buttons, folding cards, theme fade, moving accent bar.
- "Copy this look to the other modes" and "Reset this look" buttons.
- New key: Hide all overlays (Ctrl+Alt+H). Optional keys that only control the Timer or only the Countdown.
- Bigger live preview with a mini-screen showing where all three overlays sit.
- v1.0 settings are migrated automatically.

## v1.0.0
- First release.
- Clock, Timer and Countdown overlay with their own toggle keys.
- Settings window with tabs (General, Clock, Timer, Countdown, Appearance, Keys, About) and live preview.
- Custom sound for the countdown (mp3, wav, wma, m4a, aac) or a built-in beep.
- 3x3 position grid, monitor choice (game's monitor, primary, or a numbered one).
- Tray icon, start with Windows, loading screen, "Better Clock is running" message.
