# Better Clock v1.4.0 - Countdown and timer power

## New
- **Progress ring or bar:** Countdown and Timer tabs > "Progress ring / bar". The countdown empties, the timer fills towards its target.
- **Low-time colours:** the countdown turns amber (30 s left) then red (10 s left). Change the times in "Chains, Pomodoro and warnings".
- **Pulsing glow:** the countdown glows and pulses in its last 10 seconds.
- **Timer target:** the timer turns green and chimes when it reaches your target time.
- **Overtime:** the countdown keeps counting up (+00:05) after zero.
- **Chained countdowns:** e.g. `25:00 Work;5:00 Break`. The step name shows above the numbers.
- **Pomodoro mode:** work, short break and long break cycles.
- **Warning beeps** at 10, 5, 3, 2 and 1 seconds left.

## Fixed
- The Update now button now shows when a new version is available.

If you are on v1.3.0 or v1.3.1, download this version by hand once. After that, updates install from inside the app.
