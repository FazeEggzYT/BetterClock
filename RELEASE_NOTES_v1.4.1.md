# Better Clock v1.4.1 - Time features

## New
- **Alarm clock:** rings at a time you choose, shows your message on the clock and plays the countdown sound.
- **World clocks:** other cities shown as lines under the clock.
- **Countdown to a date and time**, for example a release day.
- **Days since a date.**
- **Session timer:** how long you have been playing.
- **Break reminder:** "Time for a break!" every 45 minutes (you can change it).
- **Timer results history:** the Timer tab saves each time when you press Reset.

All the clock extras are in Settings > Clock > "Alarm, world clocks, dates and breaks".
